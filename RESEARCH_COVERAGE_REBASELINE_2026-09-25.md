## Why the former percentage is suspended

The historically reported **90% comprehensive / 96% implementation-grade** values were planning judgments, not reproducible measurements. The only explicit weighted rubric in `COMPREHENSIVE_ANALYSIS_LEDGER.md` has 14 weights totaling 100, but its displayed coverage values produce **55.59%** when multiplied by those weights, while the nearby prose calls it an 82% historical snapshot. That table may have been left stale while the prose advanced; it cannot independently verify the later estimates. The old figures remain the **last historical planning estimates** until a final current-evidence rescore; they are not silently replaced with 55.59%, zero, or a function-address ratio. Function/address completion remains separately uncalibrated.

The separate [function-denominator audit](research/checkpoints/ANALYSIS_CHECKPOINT_2026-09-24_FUNCTION_DENOMINATOR_AUDIT.md) found 93,287 non-`Unwind@` entries in Ghidra and 2,528 currently curated semantic addresses. Neither is a validated count of reachable application functions. The 345-row current-server packet-source map with zero documented reachable gaps measures **source-to-document inventory**, not runtime protocol interoperability, full handler semantics or whole-client completion. Block counts, UI RTTI-name counts and asset counts likewise must not be converted into a client percentage.

| Runtime behavior and lifecycle | Frame, login/zone, object, UI, audio, space and scene paths sampled | Enumerate representative scenarios and all state transitions/failure/cleanup edges per subsystem | Bounded original-exe instruction/corpus/server evidence for each scenario, with assumptions and unknowns explicit; runtime fixtures where static evidence cannot decide |
| Function-level accounting | 2,528 curated addresses; 93,287 non-unwind analyzer entries | Build a normalized executable-function inventory: collapse thunks/CRT/library code, recover raw entries, classify reachable callbacks/vtables and confidence | Every *in-scope reachable application function* has a stable ID and at least a role/classification; deeper pseudocode only where behavior/ABI requires it. No percentage before the denominator exists |
| Engine-decision research | Engine-neutral architecture and equal-spike criteria documented | Reachability/compatibility gates below; Godot versus DX12/Vulkan remains untested | All blocking original-asset and lifecycle questions bounded enough to specify fair spikes; user then explicitly authorizes the build/spikes |

These axes are not interchangeable. The user's stronger “entire client” request requires the function-level accounting axis in addition to an engine-decision-grade behavioral model. We should report both rather than call a behavior-focused percentage “100%.”
