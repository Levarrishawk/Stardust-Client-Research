## Exact shape and provenance

`Select(generator, binding, explicitLayerIndex, policy, limits, token)` requires an accepted immutable generator and owned family binding. Policy is mandatory: `TypedEvidence` or `EnabledSupportedSlice`. The only shape is FORM LAYR/0003 with a typed inherited IHDR/0000 or 0001 header, exactly three ADTA raw words and exactly three immediate children in order BCIR/0002, FFRA/0005, AHFR/0003. Nested LAYR, extra/unknown records, other versions or opaque headers reject under both policies.

Before any layer/child export or decoder, checks establish exact cached generator hash/length, unique FractalMap role/version/group extent/count, typed layer extent, ordered contiguous child extents and exact global Rules identity. Child ordinals are 2/3/4, including the omitted IHDR/ADTA slots. Each global Rules row must match layer index, ordinal, tag, version, offset and length. The last child ends exactly at the layer tail. The accepted immutable binding already proves group bytes belong to the root. A separately decoded byte-identical generator is accepted.

The result owns encoded layer bytes, detached header/name/legacy bytes, three ADTA words and description bytes; typed BCIR; accepted FFRA/AHFR receipts; immutable child provenance and read-only child order. It retains generator/group/layer/child hashes/extents, layer index/parent/depth, child ordinals and global rule indexes. Both signed family IDs resolve exactly through accepted selectors. Their family entries retain already owned storage; missing-family fallback is absent. Exports clone buffers and the child collection rejects mutation. No generator section reference retains the original root through the slice's layer/header storage.

## Raw state and policy

`IsLocallyEnabled` preserves the inherited nonzero enabled interpretation. `BoundaryInverted` and `FilterInverted` normalize ADTA words 0/1 by nonzero, including high-bit and float-looking raw patterns. `CopyAdtaWordBits` preserves all three words. `AdtaWord2Bits` remains raw; its broader semantics are **Unknown**. Parent/depth encode containment and never establish effective activation.

Evidence policy preserves disabled and ineligible raw states while requiring the exact typed structure and both existing families. `HasSupportedInputs` reports enabled, finite BCIR geometry/serialized amount and enabled eligible FFRA/AHFR inputs independently of local layer enable. Operational policy additionally requires local layer enable and calls both selectors with their existing enabled operational policies.

The BCIR radius is not absolutized; feather selectors and serialized amounts are not repaired. FFRA threshold ordering, selector membership and serialized amount range are not added as constraints. Existing consumer rules govern those finite unusual inputs. Frozen family support remains modes0..5, curves off, bounded octaves and finite used fields. No third ADTA word meaning or ancestor admission is invented.

## Whole-slice pre-copy bounds

Defaults: layer 1048576 bytes, child 65536, name 65536, decoded 1048576, snapshot/work 8388608. Hard maxima: layer 16777216, child 4194304, name 65536, decoded 16777216, snapshot/work 134217728. All options validate before exports; child decoder limits are validated/composed from these bounds. Existing selector snapshot/work limits are capped at their hard 16777216 allowance without changing serialized state.

Let L be encoded layer length, C the sum of the three encoded child lengths and M=`3 * generator.Rules.Count + generator.Groups.Count`. Before any layer/child/name copy or decoder, reserve decoded `L +12 +C`, snapshot `2*L +12 +4*C`, and work `snapshotReservation +M`. Each child is capped separately. The whole encoded layer length is conservatively bounded by `MaximumNameBytes`, ensuring each header/description/name export fits before its length is inspected. This intentionally stricter guard can reject a smaller caller name cap even when actual names fit; the bounded slice policy therefore admits at most 65536 encoded layer bytes. No generic all-LAYR support is claimed.

Exact decoded charge is layer header/legacy/description bytes plus 12 ADTA bytes, BCIR 20 typed bytes plus its header name/legacy, and the accepted FFRA/AHFR decoded charges. Snapshot charges the owned layer, those layer decoded copies, three encoded copies for each child, child decoded payloads and temporary BCIR header-name exports used for accounting. Work adds M. These logical byte/work charges exclude CLR objects, previously owned generator/family storage, timing and later caller exports.

Cancellation brackets exports, accepted selector calls and final publication. A failure after circle/filter creation still publishes no partial slice or mutates earlier results. A 60000-byte synthetic circle name with zero aggregate snapshot cap rejects while allocating less than 16384 bytes after generator/binding construction, guarding the whole-slice pre-copy order.
