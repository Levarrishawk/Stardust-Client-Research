# Owned terrain playback selection slot

SceneWorldTerrainPlaybackSlot (external historical reference) manages replacement of the actual frozen [owned terrain playback binding](SCENE_WORLD_CURRENT_SPEED_TERRAIN_PLAYBACK_CONTRACT.md). It borrows one fixed exact world and motion store, owns its installed binding, and delegates advancement without recomputing plans, reloading readers or rebuilding retained terrain. Modern supplied-local world-Z motion remains distinct from Unknown original units, collision, movement, physics and gameplay parity.

## API and ownership

Construct `new SceneWorldTerrainPlaybackSlot(world, motions)` on the owner thread. The constructor requires an open store bound to that exact world through the accepted internal read-only check. `Current` exposes the actual installed binding or null; `ReplacementRevision` starts at zero and advances once per successful replacement. `IsDisposed` remains readable after disposal. These getters and every operation enforce owner thread and reject reentry.

`TryReplace(composition, source, surface, policy, initialTimestampMicroseconds, stepMicroseconds, footOffset = 0, terrainLimits = null, playbackLimits = null, cancellationToken = default)` calls the actual frozen `SceneWorldCurrentSpeedTerrainPlayback.Bind` with the fixed world/store and explicit caller arguments. Caller clock is explicit: replacing a completed plan at the current driver timestamp preserves a consecutive execution clock; the slot does not infer or silently rewrite timestamps. Stored speeds and all admission limits retain the frozen binding policy.

`AdvanceNext(submit, advance, token)` delegates to the actual current binding; an open empty slot returns null after cancellation admission. `IsCurrent(receipt)` asks the installed actual binding, so a prior retired binding's receipt is no longer slot-current even if its historical result remains intact. Currency is receipt/source identity, not proof that an old motion row remains latest. Callers must serialize all use and inspect exposed owned children without independently advancing, replacing or disposing them.

## Replacement sequence and effects

1. Admit owner thread/open state/cancellation and compute the next successful revision with checked unsigned arithmetic before calling Bind. Overflow therefore cannot publish a candidate. Reaching unsigned maximum through real successful replacements is not exercised; the checked branch is source-inspected.
2. Call real Bind. If it returns null or throws before successful return, preserve the installed binding reference and revision. Exceptions propagate; null maps to false. Bind admission distinguishes unavailable/stale source, exact-world/incarnation failure, closed store, invalid clocks/limits/token, and constructor grounding failure.
3. A successfully returned candidate is temporarily owned alongside the installed binding. This is an explicit synchronous two-binding construction window: the slot's reentry guard prevents slot advancement/replacement, and production dependency calls expose no asynchronous callback. No lock or cross-thread advancement is supported.
4. Recheck cancellation and composition currency. If publication cannot proceed, dispose the returned candidate in `finally` and retain the previous installed reference/revision. There is no retry.
5. Retire the previous owned binding before assigning the candidate to Current, transfer candidate ownership, then publish the prechecked revision. There is at most one installed binding. If old retirement unexpectedly throws, the candidate is disposed and the previous pointer/revision remain; its child retirement may have progressed. No restoration of a partly retired old child is claimed.

Candidate construction is not a motion-store transaction. Frozen Bind initially grounds and publishes selected motion before returning, and that publication may survive later cancellation, source change or construction failure. The old binding may remain installed while borrowed motion has changed. Neither candidate nor old disposal erases motion rows. The slot cannot reverse a publication or dispose an unreturned partially constructed Bind instance. The frozen binding's constructor-prefix limitations remain applicable. Tests exercise deterministic failures before driver publication and real successful publication/disposal; they do not force a background cancellation into the narrow post-publication window or claim a tested allocation-failure rollback.

## Retirement and lifetime

`RetireSelection()` retires Current and clears its reference in `finally`, without changing successful-replacement revision. Repeating it while open is harmless; replacement after retirement is allowed. `Dispose()` is idempotent, disposes Current and clears binding/world/store references even if child disposal throws. It closes the slot; later advances/replacements/selection retirement reject. Borrowed world, store, composition, source and surface are never disposed by the slot. Historical caller receipts remain accessible.

Source replacement/retirement and object destroy/recreate make old playback unavailable without reattaching a numerically equal object. Replacing a surface requires an explicit successful TryReplace or RetireSelection; immutable surface queries have no automatic lifetime/currentness signal. A failed changed-surface candidate leaves the old installed selection, subject to constructor-prefix effects above. Authoritative motion correction stays a borrowed-store operation and is handled by the actual driver on its next advance.

## Evidence and validation

Confirmed observations are direct ownership/reference/delegation and checked-revision control flow in the linked implementation, plus separate-assembly native scenarios (external historical reference). A focused independent verifier (external historical reference) pins two sealed frozen62 recipe hashes before importing or producing output, derives plans with the rational reference, binary32 movement/speed/pose/time independently, and Fraction barycentric terrain heights. It compares 36 steps across three precision traces with three consumed segment replacements, a changed surface height offset of two, correction Z offset of 0.125, and two further lifecycle replacements per trace. Exact position/speed/orientation/time bits match without native output seeding.

306 native checks and 75 focused negatives cover caps, overflow, pre-cancellation, late constructor grounding failure, prior owner/row/revision retention, actual replacement/source/surface/child identities, old child disposal, completed-plan replacement at current clock, foreign world with equal numeric identifiers, foreign store, closed store preserving an installed owner, explicit retirement/reuse, stale/retired source, authoritative correction, reincarnation, prior receipt currency, borrowed survival and owner thread. Reentry and post-return cancellation cleanup are source-inspected because no production callback hook is available. The earlier frozen numerical suite is not repeated.

Compatibility consequence: a host can explicitly replace one owned modern playback selection while preserving the accepted failure-prefix semantics.
