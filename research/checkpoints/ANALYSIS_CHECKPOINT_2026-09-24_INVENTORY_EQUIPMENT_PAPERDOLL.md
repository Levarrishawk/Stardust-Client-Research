Missing required right button or label takes a fatal CodeData diagnostic path. Activation `0x00E88DE0` enables the paperdoll and initializes preview state via `0x00E88E00`; deactivation at raw virtual entry `0x00E88DD0` disables it. Therefore this mediator is the equipment *presentation* panel, not by itself the item-mutation subsystem.
